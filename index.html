A Multiscale CLAHE Approach to Single Image Haze Reduction


• This paper presents a method to remove haze from a single image.
• It combines CLAHE(Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) with multiscale fusion using brightness, color, and important (saliency) areas.
• A guided image filter is used to remove noise and keep fine details clear.
• Tests on hazy images show better visibility, structure, and color quality, measured using SSIM, PSNR, and MSE
